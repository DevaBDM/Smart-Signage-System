# Conclusion and Future Work

This concluding chapter summarizes the key findings of the Smart Digital
Signage System project, maps objectives to their achieved outcomes, and 
establishes a strategic roadmap for future system evolution.

## Conclusion

This capstone project successfully designed, implemented, and validated 
an enterprise-grade Smart Digital Signage System. We have demonstrated 
that by integrating environmental intelligence, multi-tenant governance, 
and hardened network security within an open-source framework, 
institutions can achieve performance parity with proprietary platforms 
while realizing significant economic and environmental benefits.

## Immediate Priorities (0–6 months)

Immediate tasks include the transition to dedicated ARM-based edge 
hardware and fine-tuning the Weber-Fechner calibration profiles for 
diverse institutional lighting environments.

## Medium-Term Enhancements (6–18 months)

Enhancements include AI-driven predictive scheduling, integration of campus-wide 
event APIs, and the implementation of automated content integrity 
moderation.

## Research Directions (18+ months)

Future research will explore the integration of low-power Edge ML 
models for anonymous audience analytics and the design of a federated 
management architecture for geographically distributed display fleets.
